Soil-temperature columns

Every site gets every candidate soil temperature: the sensor as recorded (TS_original), step 01's repaired or reconstructed column (TS_reconstructed), what the manuscript's fits used (TS_manuscript: the regression on air temperature at 35 sites), TS_linear (TS ~ TA above freezing, fitted to the sensor) and other estimators trained only on real sensor data. Each column is qualified as step 01 qualifies its own: its growing-season start, temperature range, years kept and nighttime fit records. Estimators are scored against the sensor out of fold (each year predicted by a model trained on the others) and for plausibility against air temperature.

Where each estimator is best or close to the top 3

Number of sites at which the estimator is best, or within 10% of the third best (r²: 0.01), under each metric. An estimator that is "top" anywhere is carried forward to the fits.

Site × column

The selected metric for every site and column (grey: not available). Click a cell to open the site.
