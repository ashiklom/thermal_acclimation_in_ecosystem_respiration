The temperature sources

Soil temperature is what respiration responds to most directly, but sensors fail, drift, or are missing. The soil-temperature sources are the sensor as recorded, the repaired sensor, the default (the repaired sensor, or a regression on air temperature where the sensor is unreliable), and several statistical estimates of soil temperature from air temperature, net radiation and lagged air temperature; see Soil temperature in detail. Each is qualified as the default is (growing season, temperature range, gap screen, control year).

  • Air temperature (measured): air temperature as measured, without gap filling.
  • Air temperature (3-, 7- and 30-day running means): the mean of the measured air temperature's daily means over the last 3, 7 and 30 days: soil temperature lags air temperature, and these averages mimic that memory without a soil sensor.

Sites without any soil-temperature record have only the air-temperature sources: with a soil-temperature reference they have no comparison; choose an air-temperature reference.
