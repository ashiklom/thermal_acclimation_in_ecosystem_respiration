Published records vs the FLUXNET shuttle

The night-time NEE records the manuscript used against the FLUXNET-archive shuttle's ONEFlux products, at every site the shuttle has. FLUXNET-format sites: the published record (FLUXNET2015 and its successors, as data/site_info.csv names them) with its VUT and CUT u* filtering, against the shuttle's VUT (where the product has it) and CUT. AmeriFlux sites: the manuscript's own record (AmeriFlux BASE with its REddyProc u* threshold) against the shuttle's VUT and CUT. Every stream's night records are selected as step 01 selects them. Differences are shuttle − reference. Data: scripts/compare-flux-products.R.

FLUXNET-format reference: the published record's NEE of the same u* method (PUB_VUT vs SHUT_VUT, PUB_CUT vs SHUT_CUT). AmeriFlux reference: the manuscript's record in both comparisons (MS vs SHUT_VUT, MS vs SHUT_CUT).
