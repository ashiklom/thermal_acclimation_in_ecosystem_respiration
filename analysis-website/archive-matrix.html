TAS by temperature column

The TAS fits of every site, run with every selected soil-temperature column (each qualified as step 01 qualifies its own; see Soil temperature) and, from S22, with air temperature in the soil-temperature role. The reference is TS_manuscript, what the published fits used. Sites whose significance class changes with the column, above all where it flips between significantly negative and significantly positive, are listed first.

The air-temperature columns (S22)

Air temperature takes the soil temperature's place in both TAS models, at every site, and is qualified as the soil-temperature columns are (growing-season start, tStart/tEnd, gap screen, 2 °C floors, control year); NEE, u* filtering and the season end are step 01's. They are not ranked against the soil sensor.

  • TA_measured: air temperature as measured, without gap filling: TA_F_MDS where TA_F_MDS_QC is 0 at FLUXNET-format sites; at AmeriFlux BASE sites the air temperature step 01 reads, which has no gap-fill flag, so a PI gap-filled product (e.g. TA_PI_F) counts as measured.
  • TA_mem3d, TA_mem7d, TA_mem30d: the mean of TA_measured's daily means over the last 3, 7 and 30 days, the memory scales of the soil-temperature memory estimators. There a forest weights them against the sensor; here, with no sensor to weight against, each is a column of its own. As there, a window includes the whole current day, its later hours too.

The 13 added sites without soil temperature (*†) have only these four columns: with the soil-temperature reference they have no comparison; choose an air-temperature reference.
