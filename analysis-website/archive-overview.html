Thermal acclimation of ecosystem respiration: refactor-v2

Results of the staged refactor of workflow steps 01–02 (site data preparation and the total/direct TAS fits). Every stage is recorded in refactor-v2-log.md; this site shows the TAS estimates each saved run produced and how they differ.

Result sets

Each row is one saved run. "Changed" counts sites (present in both) whose TAS moved by at least the significance threshold relative to the published estimates; "flips" are sites whose estimate went from significantly negative to significantly positive or back (p < 0.05).
