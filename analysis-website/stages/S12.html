BUGFIX. Step 01 keeps gStart no earlier than the first day of year whose mean soil temperature is ≥ 0 °C, but min(which(TS >= 0)) returned that day's row in the day-of-year climatology. Row and day of year differ where the climatology does not start at day 1 (southern-hemisphere sites, whose day of year runs from 183) or has missing days. gStart also bounds the step-04 scenario windows.

Result. No site is affected: wherever the row and the day of year differ (the wrapped southern-hemisphere sites), the bound does not bind, so every growing-season start, input and TAS is unchanged. The fix only matters for data where it would bind.
