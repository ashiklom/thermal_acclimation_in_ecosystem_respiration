BREAKING. tStart and tEnd bound the soil temperatures over which TAS is estimated: a 14-day window is skipped when its mean soil temperature falls outside [max(tStart, 2 °C), tEnd]. Step 01 sets them from the site's climatology: the 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles of the day-of-year mean soil temperature over the days whose mean NEE marks the growing season (floored at 0 °C, and at 2 °C at CH-Dav, US-Ha1 and US-GLE). At the 35 sites whose soil temperature step 02 replaces by a regression on air temperature, step 02 then recomputed them differently: 2.5/97.5% quantiles of the half-hourly regressed TS within gStart..gEnd. That range is wider than the climatology's, and it dropped US-GLE's 2 °C floor.

Now every site uses step 01's rule, applied to the soil temperature the fits use: at the regression sites, the regression applied to the climatology record's air temperature. The non-regression sites' inputs are unchanged.

Result. The inputs change at exactly the 35 regression sites, and only in tStart and tEnd: the climatology's range is narrower (tEnd lower by 3–8 °C, tStart mostly higher). But tStart and tEnd only decide which windows are skipped, and at 34 of the 35 sites no window's mean soil temperature crosses the new bounds, so their fits are identical. At BE-Dor 3 of 14 windows now fall outside the range; its TAS rises (total 0.011 → 0.018, direct 0.013 → 0.018) and becomes significantly positive in both models. No sign flips.
